One single-line joke about office life, written by a small deterministic program to `/output/entry.txt`, for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes" bounty.

## The joke

> I hit reply-all asking to be removed from the thread, and now I run a 400-person support group for people who hit reply-all.

Why this line: it is a genuine one-line office joke (reply-all, a truth every office worker recognises with a wince), it turns at the end — asking to escape the thread lands the speaker in charge of the very people he was fleeing — it is tight at 124 characters, and it takes a fresh angle instead of the obvious Monday-morning or photocopier line.
